Sudewa Hettiarachchi

Founder / Chief Executive Officer

A seasoned media specialist with over 25 years of experience in journalism, broadcasting and media management. An award-winning professional with both local and international recognition, he has held key leadership roles including Director of News and Current Affairs at ABC Radio, CEO of EBC Broadcasting, and Director General of the President's Media Division. Founder / Chief Strategy Officer

A pioneering marketing strategist and media visionary with over two decades of experience in brand and communication leadership. His innovative strategies have driven remarkable growth and earned him international recognition, including the International Brand Leadership Award and Media Professional of the Year.
